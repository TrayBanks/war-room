Deep dive: their lineup and how the lock line works

Each starter counts one game a week. After a game you can lock it, but only before his next game tips; if you don't, his last game of the week counts.

His line is what waiting is worth: the games he has left, counting the chance he sits. At or above the line: lock. Below: wait. With one game left the line is about his average.

The lock calls check Sleeper every 2 minutes while this page is open. Sleeper doesn't report locks until a player plays again, so after you lock someone in Sleeper, tap "I locked this".
